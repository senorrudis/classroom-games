FIELD NOTES

AIM HIGH.
BOUNCE FARTHER.

Choose a flyer. Williams is balanced. Rudis brings musical notes and a Power Chord. Hinkle has balloon-like bounces and a much more spacious course.

Aim + power. Set the sliders, then tap Launch. Or hold to charge and release to fire. Try 35–45° to start.

One boost each flight. The launch button turns blue. Tap it or press Space for extra lift and speed. Time it just before a low obstacle.

Watch the objects. Homework Mountain and Dismissal Gridlock end the run with soft landings. Phones slow you down. Backpacks bounce or slow you. Gym mats give you a big boost.

Hinkle gets room to bounce. His first object is farther away, and his obstacle spacing is 2.65 times the normal gap, plus extra room before stopping hazards. Records are separate for each character.

Keyboard: Left / Right = angle; Up / Down = power; Space = launch / boost; R = retry; N = new field; P = pause; M = sound. Distance uses game yards on an extended field.
